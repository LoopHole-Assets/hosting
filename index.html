Who We Are

A software team that understands business, not just code

Loophole InfoTech is a software development company and SaaS product maker based in Kathmandu, Nepal. We work with hotels, restaurants, retail stores, and service businesses that want to spend less time on paperwork and more time serving their customers.

Our journey started with a simple observation: most businesses in Nepal were running on registers, spreadsheets, and guesswork. So we built software that replaces the chaos with clarity — tools that handle daily operations, keep records clean, and show owners exactly what's happening in their business.

Why Loophole

Why businesses choose to work with us

Three principles guide every line of code we write and every conversation we have.

Client-First Thinking

We start with your problem, not our toolbox. Before writing a single line of code, we sit with you, watch how your business runs, and ask the questions that uncover what actually needs fixing. The result is software your team adopts on day one — because it was built for them, not for a demo.

Innovation-Driven

Technology moves fast, and businesses that move with it win. We keep our products and our skills current — modern frameworks, clean architecture, and features that solve tomorrow's problems, not just yesterday's. When you build with us, you're not buying software that expires; you're investing in a platform that improves.

Nepal-Built, Ready for the World

We understand local businesses because we are one — the currencies, the taxes, the rush hours, the power cuts that shaped how companies really operate. That understanding is baked into our products, and it travels well: the same quality standards we build for Kathmandu meet what clients anywhere in the world expect.

The Difference

What our software does for your business

The outcomes our clients care about — and the ones we build toward every day.

Save Time

Bills that took five minutes now take thirty seconds. Reports that needed a full evening generate themselves. When repetitive work runs on autopilot, your team gets hours back every single day — and those hours go into serving customers, not fighting paperwork.

Stay Organized

Every booking, order, bill, and stock movement lives in one organized place. No more overflowing registers, missing receipts, or "who changed this?" mysteries. Records stay clean, searchable, and exactly where you expect them to be when you need them.

Understand Your Business

Which item sells fastest? Which day of the week earns the most? Where is money quietly leaking? Live dashboards and clear reports turn your daily records into answers — so your next decision is based on facts, not gut feeling alone.

Grow with Confidence

Growth breaks weak systems. Ours are built to scale — from one counter to many branches, from ten orders a day to hundreds. Add locations, add staff, add new services; the software grows alongside your ambition without needing to be replaced.
