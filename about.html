About Us

The team behind software that businesses rely on

We're a Kathmandu-based software company with one focus: building practical tools that help real businesses run better every day.

Our Story

Started with a frustration. Grew into a mission.

Loophole InfoTech began with a familiar scene: a restaurant owner buried under paper order slips at the end of a long night, trying to match bills with a calculator and a fading memory. That moment — repeated in hotels, shops, and cafÃ©s all over Kathmandu — was the spark.

We asked a simple question: why should small and growing businesses run blind when software could give them clear eyes? Our first product was built for exactly that. Then came the next one, and the next — each shaped by long conversations with the people who would actually use it, from front-desk staff to kitchen chefs to shop owners.

Today, we're a full software company. We build our own products — HMS for hotels, RMS for restaurants, and our Retail Management System for stores — and we design custom software for businesses whose needs don't fit off-the-shelf tools. Different products, one philosophy: software should make the workday simpler, not more complicated.

Built in Kathmandu.Our RMS product serving restaurants across Nepal

Practical Innovation

We love new technology, but we never chase trends for their own sake. Every feature we ship has to earn its place by solving a real problem that a real business feels. If it doesn't save time, prevent loss, or make a decision easier — it doesn't make the cut.

Built Around People

Software is only successful when the people using it actually like it. That's why we design for the cashier on a busy Saturday, the waiter with full hands, and the owner checking numbers from home — not just for a polished demo. Simple screens, clear language, patient training.

Always Improving

Launching is the beginning, not the end. We release updates based on client feedback, fix issues fast, and keep refining until the software feels effortless. Our clients' daily frustrations are our product roadmap — and it's the most honest roadmap we could ask for.

Our Approach

How we take a project from idea to impact

A simple, transparent process — you'll always know what's happening and what comes next.

1

Understand

We sit with you and your team, watch how the work actually flows, and map the problems worth solving. No assumptions — we listen until we can explain your business back to you.

2

Plan

We turn findings into a clear scope, realistic timeline, and honest budget. You'll see exactly what will be built, in what order, and why — before any code is written.

3

Build

We develop in short cycles with regular demos, so you see progress early and often. Your feedback shapes the product as it grows — not after it's finished.

4

Improve

After launch, we stay close: training, support, and updates driven by real usage. The software keeps getting better because we keep listening.
